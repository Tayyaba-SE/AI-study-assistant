About

AI Study Assistant

A student-focused AI platform designed to help with learning, programming, revision, and study planning.

Version 1.0.0 · Built with Flask, the Google GenAI SDK, and the Gemini API.

Help & support

Answers to common questions about using StudyAI.

Type a question into the message box at the bottom of the screen and press Enter or tap send. Your conversation is saved automatically under Chat history.

Tap the note icon on any AI response to save it, or open Saved Notes from the sidebar and write one directly.

Open Study Planner from the sidebar. Generate a plan with AI by describing what you need to study, or add tasks manually, then check them off as you complete them.

Open Quiz generator from the sidebar, enter a topic and your preferred settings, then click Generate quiz.

Open Flashcards from the sidebar, enter a topic, and click Generate flashcards. Use Save deck to keep any set you want to study again later.

Open Chat history from the sidebar — every conversation is saved automatically as you chat, and survives a page refresh.

Open Profile from the sidebar or the profile icon in the top-right corner, click Edit profile, make your changes, then click Save changes.

Open Settings from the sidebar to switch between light and dark mode, adjust AI response preferences, or clear stored data.

Contact & feedback

This is a local student project without a live support inbox yet. In the meantime, use the thumbs up / down icons on any AI response to flag feedback, or jot down issues in a Saved Note.

StudyAI can make mistakes. Double-check important answers.
